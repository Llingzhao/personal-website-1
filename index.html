Ling Zhao

PhD student in Biology at Temple University, Yang Lab.

I am interested in how the nuclear pore complex — the cell's gateway between the nucleus and the cytoplasm — is organized at the nanoscale, and how that organization shapes the transport of molecules through it.

My Research About Me

Hello, and welcome.

I am a PhD student in the Biology program at Temple University in Philadelphia, where I recently joined the Yang Lab. My research interests center on the nuclear pore complex (NPC): how FG-nucleoporins are organized inside individual pores, and how that nanoscale organization relates to selective transport between the nucleus and the cytoplasm.

Before coming to Temple, I trained in optometry and worked in ophthalmic clinical data analysis and diagnostic laboratory settings — experience that gave me a lasting appreciation for careful imaging, quantitative analysis, and reproducible work.

Research Interests
